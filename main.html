Dear Faghia,

Let's be honest — we fight. A lot. We snap at each other in the middle of exam season because we're fried and frustrated, halfway through a bowl of noodles, mid-gossip about which university boy did what this week. I still haven't let go of the "come on, chalo, let's go to Mam for attendance" when I was too lazy to move — I minded that, and you know I did.

And the shoes. You said "pass me the shoes," I walked back in from class drenched in sweat, no light in the room, didn't pass them fast enough — and you shut the door on me. Furious. Absolutely iconic. I'm still a little angry about it and I will bring it up again, forever.

And yet — through every single fight, we love each other more. That's the whole point of us. We watch K-dramas till our eyes give up, swear on our lives we're never getting married, and rant about uni boys and chapri types like it's a competitive sport. Some days you make Maggie for me, I make chai for you, and we just… gossip about absolutely everyone.

You're my favourite person to fight with and the last person I'd ever trade. Happy birthday, you dramatic, impossible, wonderful human. 💜
